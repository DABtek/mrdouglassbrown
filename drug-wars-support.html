Drug Wars · Support

Help & questions.

A neon remake of the 1984 pocket-computer trading classic. Everything you need to get going — and how to reach a real person if you're stuck.

How to play

Prices swing every day and every city. Buy what's dirt cheap, carry it somewhere it's running hot, and pocket the spread. Every trip burns days — when the clock hits closing time, whatever cash you're holding is your final score.

  • Mind the shark. You start owing the loan shark at 10% a day. Pay it down fast.
  • Upgrade your pack so you can carry more each trip.
  • The world opens up after the block — source cheap overseas, sell into hungry markets.
  • Pick your run: Quick (20 days), Standard (30), or Long (45). Each keeps its own high score.

Tap HOW TO PLAY on the title screen anytime for the full walkthrough.

Frequently asked

  • Is it free? Act I is free, with no ads and no account. One in-app purchase ($2.99, one time) unlocks Acts II and III and the leaderboard. Bought it already? Tap Restore Purchase on the unlock screen.
  • Does it need the internet? Only to buy or restore the full game. Play itself is fully offline.
  • Does it collect my data? No — see the privacy policy. Nothing leaves your phone.
  • Where are my scores saved? On your device only. Deleting the app clears them.
  • Is it really the old game? It's an original remake of the 1984 pocket-computer classic — same loop, modern build.

Drug Wars · Privacy

Nothing leaves your phone.

Drug Wars is a single-player game with no account, no server, and no tracking. Here is exactly what that means, in plain language.

Last updated: July 28, 2026

The short version

Drug Wars collects no personal information. There is no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, and no network connection. The game runs entirely on your device. We could not see your data even if we wanted to — there is nowhere for it to go.

What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. We do not use cookies, advertising identifiers, location, contacts, the camera, the microphone, or any device sensors.

What the app stores on your device

The only thing Drug Wars saves is your game progress — your high scores for each run length and whether you've seen the how-to screen. This is kept in the app's own local storage on your iPhone. It never leaves the device, and it is removed when you delete the app.

No third parties

Drug Wars contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Children

Drug Wars is rated for adults and is not directed to children. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Because the app collects nothing, we don't anticipate material changes.
